engine-low.wav, engine-mid.wav, engine-high.wav

Cut from "Large car interior start & drive; speeds" in the USC Optical Sound Effects
Library (SSE Library: VEHICLES), https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_VEHICLES
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

One car, one microphone, three revs, so crossfading them never changes instrument.
Each loop is trimmed to a whole number of firing cycles with a wrap-around crossfade
and resampled to 16 kHz by scripts/bolides-eng.mjs.
