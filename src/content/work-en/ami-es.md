<p>
  AMI·ES is a mobile app that supports autistic students and students with neurodevelopmental conditions through university life. It was designed together with Claude Bernard Lyon 1 University.
</p>
<p>
  Everything is built around accessibility, predictability and cognitive load: a clear, customisable interface that adapts to each person's needs.
</p>
<h3>Main features</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Visual timer</strong> — a coloured dial that makes passing time tangible and easier to read.</li>
  <li><strong>Energy management</strong> — track the energy available across the day, with alert thresholds and break reminders.</li>
  <li><strong>Adapted planner</strong> — a readable timetable that shows the energy cost of each activity.</li>
  <li><strong>Ready-made messages</strong> — prewritten sentences to make communication easier.</li>
  <li><strong>Campus information</strong> — one place for the university's useful resources and documents.</li>
  <li><strong>To-do lists &amp; useful apps</strong> — tools to break down and organise everyday life.</li>
</ul>
<h3>And more to come</h3>
<p>
  The app is under active development and keeps growing. Coming next:
</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Activity reviews</strong> — an overview to step back and look at your days and your progress.</li>
  <li><strong>My garden</strong> — a playful space that rewards effort and keeps motivation up.</li>
  <li><strong>Reflection time</strong> — a moment to check in on how you feel and what you need.</li>
  <li><strong>Social side</strong> — stay in touch with peers and support contacts.</li>
</ul>
<p>
  Built with Unity, shipped on Android and iOS.
</p>
