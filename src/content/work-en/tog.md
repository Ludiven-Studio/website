<p>
  Play a privateer in Trails of Gold. Sail waters full of mythical creatures and haul in as much treasure as you can to become a true legend of the seas!
</p>
<div>
  <p>
    <ul>
      <li>Game built by <a href ="https://www.linkedin.com/company/la-suite-studio/" target="_blank">La Suite Studio</a>
      <li>Available on <a href ="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1804440/Trails_Of_Gold_Privateers/" target="_blank">Steam</a>
      <li>A VR title running in French arcades such as <a href ="https://www.multiworld.com/jeux/77/trails-of-gold/" target="_blank"> MultiWorld</a> and <a href ="https://ereel.fr/experiences/shooting/trail-of-gold/" target="_blank"> EReel</a>

      <li>Multiplayer handled with <a href ="https://www.photonengine.com/#" target="_blank"> Photon</a>
      <li>Avatars driven by the <a href ="http://www.root-motion.com/finalikdox/html/page16.html" target="_blank"> VRIK</a> system
      <li>Built an "Endless" mode for open-ended sessions
      <li>Built a theme system for Halloween and Christmas
      <li>Level unlocks and tutorials for the Steam release
      <li>Added a new weapon and new enemies
      <li>Improved teleportation with anchor points
      <li>Support for up to 16 players (8 vs 8)
      <li>End-of-game screens with PDF export
