<p>
  Join Serena on her spellbinding journey and work out how to defeat the evil that dried up the magic river. Mix and match potions, cast spells and chain long paths to grow Serena's power and clear the levels.
  The goal is to draw lines through potions of the same colour — the longer the line, the more bonuses can appear.
</p>
